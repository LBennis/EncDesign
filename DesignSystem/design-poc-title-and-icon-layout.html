Title shrink-wrap

Each title is clamped to 3 lines with an action beside it. On the left the h3 keeps its full width when it wraps. On the right, JS measures the widest visible line and sets the h3 to that width, so the action sits next to the text. Drag the width slider to watch it reflow.

Dead space (hatched) is the gap between the end of the longest line and the edge of the h3 box. The fix measures line boxes with Range.getClientRects(), ignores lines hidden by the clamp, and re-runs through a ResizeObserver on the wrapper.
